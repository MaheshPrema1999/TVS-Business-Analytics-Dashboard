TVS Sales & Delivery Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview:
This project analyzes TVS Motors sales and delivery operations using SQL and Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, dealer performance, bike model sales, and delivery efficiency to support business decision-making.

Business Problem:
TVS Motors required a centralized reporting solution to monitor sales performance, dealer contributions, bike model demand, and delivery operations.

The objective was to:
1.Track overall sales performance
2.Identify top-performing dealers
3.Analyze popular bike models
4.Monitor delivery efficiency
5.Detect delivery delays and operational bottlenecks
